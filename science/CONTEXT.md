# Lagoon Intelligence Platform (LIP)

Version: 1.0

## Mission

Build the leading lagoon management decision-support platform for the UAE and GCC.

The platform must evolve from:

Water Quality Monitoring
→ Predictive Lagoon Management
→ Lagoon Digital Twin

The platform shall help operators:

- Prevent eutrophication
- Prevent cyanobacterial blooms
- Identify nutrient sources
- Forecast lagoon health
- Prioritize interventions
- Demonstrate compliance

The platform is not a dashboard.

It is a scientific decision-support system.

## Core Scientific Model

Nutrient Sources
↓
Hydraulic Transport
↓
Sediment Interactions
↓
Fe-P Coupling
↓
Bloom Formation
↓
Operational Risk

Every future module should fit somewhere in this chain.
